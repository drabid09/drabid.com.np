About

Security engineer turned trainer. Still both.

I spent almost five years at SecurityPal leading customer operations for enterprise clients, many of them Fortune 500 companies. The work covered vendor security assessments, InfoSec and AI policy, FedRAMP and StateRAMP, and security due diligence.

Before that I worked in marketing, business development and events, much of it through AIESEC in Nepal. That's where I learned to hold a room.

Today I build and deliver AI training for NAAMII under the EU UDAAN programme, and run sessions for colleges, youth programmes and founders. What I care about most is people development: everyone should leave with something they can do on Monday.
